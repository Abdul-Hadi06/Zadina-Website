Kholas

Khalas dates are primarily grown in the UAE and Saudi Arabia. They are medium-sized with reddish-golden brown skin and a moist, tender, chewy texture.

What is the nutritional content of Khalas dates (per 100g)? * Calories: ~330 kcal ,Carbohydrates: ~80–87g (~70g natural sugars) ,Fiber: ~7g , Protein: ~2–3g , Fat: ~1.5g , Vitamins/Minerals: Rich in potassium, magnesium, iron, and antioxidants.

They are also excellent stuffed with roasted pecans or blended into creamy milkshakes.

Khidri

Khidri dates are elongated with glossy dark brown to black skin. They are known for being durable during transit and having a pleasantly chewy consistency.

* Calories: ~310 kcal , Carbohydrates: ~75–80g , Protein: ~3g , Vitamins/Minerals: Rich in fiber, potassium, magnesium, calcium, and antioxidants. Their firm texture makes them ideal for chopping into spiced rice dishes or pilaf. They also pair perfectly with almond butter or tahini.

Bumaan

Cultivated in the date groves of Al Ain, Bumaan dates are small and oval with tender, semi-moist flesh. They offer a gentle sweetness with a soft, mild, and subtly nutty profile that appeals to both traditional and modern palates.

Calories: ~280 kcal
Carbohydrates: ~75g , Fiber: ~7g ,Protein: ~2g , Fat: Negligible
Vitamins/Minerals: Rich in potassium, iron, calcium, and antioxidants

They also make elegant appetizers when stuffed with soft cheese.

Fard

Originally from Oman, they are now widely grown in Al Ain, Dubai, and Fujairah. They are known for their deep, chocolate-brown skin and firm, semi-moist flesh.

They have a dense bite with a gentle honey-caramel sweetness. Notably, they are resilient and long-lasting, maintaining their flavor even after months of storage.

*Calories: ~319 kcal , Carbohydrates: ~80g , Protein: ~2.8g , Fat: Minimal,
Vitamins/Minerals: Packed with fiber, calcium, magnesium, potassium, vitamin C, and antioxidants.
They can also be stewed with cinnamon for a fragrant dessert.

Lulu

Lulu dates are grown in the warm deserts of the Northern United Arab Emirates. They are petite, round, and glossy, offering a soft, tender, melt-in-your-mouth feel that has made them a household favorite for generations.

These dates are bursting with a rich caramel-honey sweetness, making them an indulgent yet wholesome choice.

Calories: ~282 kcal , Carbohydrates: ~75g (mostly natural sugars) , Protein: ~2g , Fat: ~0.5g ,
Vitamins/Minerals: Naturally high in dietary fiber, potassium, and antioxidants.

For a gourmet twist, try stuffing them with almonds, pistachios, or cream cheese, or adding them to baked goods for natural sweetness.

Ajwa

Hailing from Madinah, Saudi Arabia, Ajwa dates are small and almost black. They have a fine, soft, and tender texture.

They have a unique, mild sweetness blended with subtle hints of cinnamon and prune.

*Calories: ~300 kcal ,Carbohydrates: ~75g , Protein: ~2g ,
Vitamins/Minerals: High in fiber, potassium, magnesium, calcium, and antioxidants.

Enjoy them as a slow-chew treat on their own, or serve them with warm herbal tea to complement their spiced notes. They also add a nice hint of spice to morning oatmeal.

Medjool

They are prized for their impressive large size and juicy, plump texture. Originally from Morocco, they are now grown in Jordan and the USA.

Medjool dates have amber-brown skin and tender flesh that delivers an intense caramel and honey flavor.

Calories: ~277 kcal , Carbohydrates: ~75g , Protein: ~1.8g
Vitamins/Minerals: High in fiber, potassium, magnesium, calcium, and antioxidants

They can also be used in baking to completely replace refined sugar with natural sweetness.
